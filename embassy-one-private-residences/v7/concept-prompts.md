# Embassy ONE Sales Suite — Home Screen Concept Prompts

Image-gen prompts for finalizing the home-screen concept. Generate, compare, pick a
direction (or fuse). **No build until the direction is called.**

## Design DNA (locked from `Files for Agency 2/`)
- **Signature motif:** translucent sage-green agave/aloe leaf silhouette bleeding from
  edges (on nearly every ID page).
- **Type:** widely letter-spaced high-contrast serif ALL-CAPS + flowing script accent +
  thin sans labels.
- **Structure:** numbered sections 01–06, thin brass hairlines, vast warm-white space.
- **Palette:** bone/cream · travertine beige · warm walnut · terracotta-clay · cream
  boucle · soft sage · aged brass. ("Contemporary Refinement — Elegant, Calm, Timeless.")
- **Landscape language:** colored masterplan zoning plans, curved jogging-loop linework,
  botanical tree studies, circular seasonal colour-wheel (spring/summer/monsoon/autumn),
  frosted-glass cube lantern (day = botanical light; dusk = deep blue + seasonal glow).
- **Five Lanterns:** Winter/Spring/Summer/Autumn + Center — the hero spatial+seasonal device.

## Shared style suffix (append to any prompt)
> Ultra-luxury real-estate sales application UI, high-fidelity interface design mockup,
> landscape 16:9. Palette: warm bone/stone off-white, soft sage green, aged brass
> hairlines, walnut timber, frosted-glass white, deep twilight blue. Spaced wide
> ALL-CAPS high-contrast serif wordmark + thin script accent + quiet sans labels.
> Generous negative space, calm, cinematic, museum-grade restraint, soft natural light,
> subtle grain. NO clutter, no neon, no purple gradients, no glitter/gold excess, no
> busy charts, no urgency badges, no price tags. Architectural Digest × Four Seasons quiet.

## D — The Season Dial  (rotate through seasons)  ⭐ NEW
Centered on warm bone paper, a large elegant circular dial like a refined brass-and-stone
compass, divided into four seasonal arcs (spring pink, summer gold, monsoon blue, autumn
coral) with a glowing frosted-glass cube lantern at its hub. Behind it, a soft full-bleed
garden matching the selected season, dawn-to-dusk gradient. Five luminous lantern nodes
around the ring. Spaced serif "EMBASSY ONE" top-centre, script subtitle "the residences".
Translucent sage agave-leaf bleeding from lower corners. Brass hairline frame. Small-caps
nav around the dial: THE PLACE · THE GROUNDS · THE TOWER · THE RESIDENCES.
- **Buttons:** season arcs glow + bloom on touch; rotating the ring crossfades the world
  between seasons and day↔dusk; the lantern hub is "enter" — tap, it brightens, dial

## F — The Drift (cinematic living film)  ⭐ NEW · uses VIDEO
Home screen IS a slow ambient film: golden-dusk drone drift over the tower → through
gardens → past glowing lanterns, seamless loop. Floating frosted-glass UI over the
moving image. Spaced serif "EMBASSY ONE" top-centre + whispered script line; a row of
four frosted-glass "lantern" nav chips lower-third (THE PLACE · THE GROUNDS · THE TOWER
· THE RESIDENCES), translucent backlit glass + brass edge + soft glow. Sage agave-leaf
in a corner. Film grain, drift motion-blur.
- **Build (kd):** full-bleed looping muted HTML5 video bg + backdrop-filter glass buttons
  + kd-motion float/ripple; section enter crossfades to that section's loop; letterboxed.
- **Buttons:** glass lantern chips catch the moving light, frost-brighten + ripple on touch.
- **Assets to request:** 1 seamless 15–30s hero loop (dusk drone→garden→lantern), silent;
  optional 4 short per-section loops; posters cut from video.

## G — The Threshold (layered parallax diorama)  ⭐ NEW · uses LAYERED IMAGES
Home is a living shadow-box with depth: translucent agave leaves (foreground), glowing
lantern + garden (mid), slender tower against twilight (back); layers drift at different
speeds on tilt (iPad gyro) / pointer (kiosk). Nav = four tall "threshold" panels
(folding-screen doorways) standing in the scene, frosted + brass edge + small-caps label.
"Khosha = layers" made literal. Volumetric dusk, parallax depth.
- **Build (kd + design-as-layers):** transparent PNG layers parallaxed via pointer/gyro
  transforms; CSS depth haze; kd-motion drift + threshold-panel open reveal.
- **Buttons:** threshold panels brighten, haze parts, panel swings open into the room;
  foreground leaves blur past on enter.
- **Assets to request:** scene as 3–4 transparent PNG layers (fg agave leaves · lantern+
  garden mid · tower+sky bg), or one composition to slice.
